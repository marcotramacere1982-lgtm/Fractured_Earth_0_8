## 3-1. The entities and the corporate cartel {#sec-3-1}

The modern landscape is not governed by states, but by consolidated supply chains, data monopolies, and logistics networks. These factions do not seek ideological dominance; they seek absolute predictability, algorithmic optimization, and the elimination of human friction.

**THE INFRASTRUCTURE & LOGISTICS MONOPOLIES**

***The Euro-Grid Syndicate (The Grid)***

The monolithic utility cartel that owns, maintains, and throttles the physical power lines, global fiber-optic trunks, and data carrier waves. They are the architects of the background static—the constant, low-frequency industrial fan whine and electrical tinnitus that hums through every windowless server farm and brick tenement.

The Footprint: Their presence is felt through the Optic-Grids and automated digital tollbooths encrusting the leftover infrastructure of the late 20th century.

Systemic Grip: The Syndicate uses Anomalous Throttling and firewalls to manage data flow. When a region's economic output dips or security threats are detected, they deploy Network Throttling, cutting off cloud connectivity and plunging entire city blocks into an un-networked dark state to isolate anomalies.

![Typical transport hub.](images/corpos.webp)

***The Avalon-Collins Liquidity Ledger (The Ledger)***

The undisputed spine of global physical distribution and day-to-day transaction processing. Born from the total merger of automated e-commerce networks and international freight carriers, the Ledger owns the physical concrete roads and the digital transactions that cross them.

The Footprint: They operate the vast Industrial Logistics Grids—the kilometers of grey corrugated steel fulfillment centers and automated drone hives that swallow regional landscapes.

Systemic Grip: They control the Avalon-Collins Stables, the corporate-backed digital fiat used by the middle and lower classes to pay for synthetic food and tenement rent. If your profile is flagged by a Ledger algorithm, your digital wallet is frozen at the turnstile, effectively air-gapping you from physical survival.

**THE PRIVATE SECURITY AND THE LIQUIDATION BLOCKS**

***Centurion-Vanguard Holdings ("The Watch")***

The massive, low-cost security conglomerate contracted by municipal bodies and logistics giants to police the Dense Urban canyons and regional transit lines. They treat security as a low-margin asset optimization problem.

- The Footprint: They field the Tier 1 Private Contract Guards—overworked, underpaid individuals who rely entirely on the local AI subnet to tell them where to walk, what to log, and who to audit.

Systemic Grip: While their human guards are easily bypassed by a disciplined Gaze-Brake or a well-planned Flashback, they are backed by the inescapable tracking data of the Hive-Mind Subnet. They generate System Threat Points the moment a Freelancer makes an uncooled noise or leaves a digital trace.

***Obsidian Response Group (The TRF Squads)***

The elite, privatized tactical cleanup branch used exclusively by the high-tier corporate boards when a facility reaches Terminal Lockdown. They do not engage in standard policing; they are asset recovery and systemic hazard elimination units.

- The Footprint: Their operatives are the Tier 2 Tactical Reaction Forces (TRF). Hidden behind sealed tactical respirators with integrated amber AR lenses, they move with a terrifying, synchronized physical discipline.

Systemic Grip: When Obsidian is deployed at Escalation Level 3, all local public cellular handshakes are terminated. They hunt in 4-man units, utilizing specialized kinetic weapons, tracking drones, and frequency dampeners specifically designed to force an operator's hardware into a hard firmware glitch.

## Corporations: beneath the surface

> Corpos may look like monolithic giants from the outside, with one voice, and absolute clarity of intent. But the ecosystem complexity, the internal politics, the power struggles and career aspirations of hundreds of thousands of individuals create fertile ground for friction, delayed projects, backstabbing, fights, project delays and resource competition. So, while the corporative grip is almost absolute nowadays, reality is nuanced and there is leverage to influence everyone, from top-dogs to lower ranks.

> Not to mention - fractures do happen. The corporations that we have today rose from acquisitions, mergers and divisions from old companies. These are not eternal entities, and the human nature behind is still a driving engine.

> To the trained eye, corporations are much less stable than what they want to project.

**THE GREY ZONES' FACTIONS**

***The Un-Networked Operators (The Air-Gappers)***

A loose, paranoid underground network of former process operators, line laborers, and rogue tech-splicers who have chosen total systemic disconnection over monitored compliance.

- The Footprint: They inhabit the Grey Zones—the unmonitored architectural blind spots, abandoned transit tunnels, and gutted main street storefronts where wireless signals fail.

- Systemic Grip: They are the inventors of the Freelancer Street Glossary and the defensive Quirks of the pavement. They deal strictly in Street Barter—trading physical assets like pre-war scrap copper wiring, antibiotic blister packs, and water tablets. They operate entirely via physical 40-pin umbilical cables to keep their customized, air-gapped companion drones safe from remote corporate intrusion.

***The Hum-Singers***

An migratory, loosely organized counter-cultural movement of acoustic street musicians, grid-drifters, and radical preservationists who use low-tech analog tools to disrupt corporate audio profiling.

- The Footprint: They are found on the dark street levels of Dense Urban zones or hanging around the overgrown public squares of Small Urban hubs.

Systemic Grip: They build and play heavy, un-amplified acoustic instruments. By performing long, repeating, low-frequency folk melodies, they create a localized acoustic shadow that masks the maddening tinnitus of the regional grid, allowing unmonitored face-to-face conversations to occur in high-surveillance public areas without triggering an AI subnet alert.

## 3-2. Dense Urban Layer (The Multi-Tier Squeeze) {#sec-3-2}

![A typical street in modern days.](images/homes_high_density_exterior.webp)

A vertical architectural sandwich where human beings are packed into high-density zones. At the street level, life exists in a dark, choked canyon completely eclipsed by overhanging scaffolding, exposed fiber-optic trunk lines, and heavy corrugated plastic awnings. These awnings are not for the weather; they are designed to protect pedestrians from the chemical grease and gray water constantly dripping from the pristine corporate tiers high above. Rusted brick tenements line the alleys, illuminated only by the harsh, flickering, high-contrast glare of massive digital billboards mounted directly onto the crumbling brickwork.

## 3-3. Small Urban Layer (The Stagnant Hubs) {#sec-3-3}

![A typical street in modern days.](images/streets_neglected_2.webp)

These are the smaller regional transport hubs left behind by economic centralization. They are defined by a eerie, slow-motion decay of municipal infrastructure. Public parks are choked with weeds, blocked off by rusted chain-link fence lines marked with city bankruptcy notices. Main street storefronts have been completely gutted of human operators; the street level consists entirely of automated parcel pickup lockers, unstaffed digital medical-diagnostic kiosks, and windowless, gray concrete server farms that emit a constant, depressing industrial fan whine into the damp air.

## 3-4. Industrial Layer (The Modular Logistics Grid) {#sec-3-4}

![A typical street in modern days.](images/corpos.webp)

Kilometers of continuous, grey corrugated steel fulfillment centers, automated drone hives, and freight sorting facilities. There is no human scale here, and there are absolutely no human pedestrians. The landscape is a flat, monochrome grid of thick concrete access roads traversed solely by heavy, automated corporate freight trucks. The handful of human technicians and supervisors required to keep the lines clear do not commute; they live permanently on-site in modular, stackable shipping-container pods anchored to the gravel lots just inside the automated perimeter fence lines.

## 3-5. Rural Layer (The Automated Mono-Crop) {#sec-3-5}

![A typical street in modern days.](images/crops.webp)

The countryside has been entirely purged of its romantic, agrarian past, replaced by vast landscapes of corporate agricultural optimization. Fields stretch out to the horizon, perfectly uniform, flat, and managed with mathematical precision by automated, GPS-guided tractors and chemical-misting drones. Regular citizens are legally barred from setting foot within these agricultural sectors due to strict corporate intellectual property and biosecurity laws. Driving down the state-maintained highways reveals an eerie, dead landscape: zero human souls, just vast, silent oceans of patented wheat and tall, black security camera posts tracking the dust.
